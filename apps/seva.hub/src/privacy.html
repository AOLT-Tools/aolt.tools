Seva Hub Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Seva Hub is a community platform for Art of Living volunteers and participants. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Seva Hub.

Information we collect

When you choose Google Sign-In, Seva Hub may receive basic Google account information such as your name and email address. Authorized users may also enter participant, program, follow-up, event, or volunteer information while using community-management features.

Why we use Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate authorized users, verify account access, and personalize the Seva Hub workspace. Basic Google account information is used for these sign-in and account-management purposes.

How information is used

Information is used to provide Seva Hub functionality, coordinate community and program activities, manage authorized access, maintain service security, and support user-requested features.

Google API data

Seva Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and sale of information

Seva Hub does not sell Google user data. Information may be shared only when necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable law, protect users or the service, or when sharing has been authorized by the user.

Data storage and security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information handled by Seva Hub. Access to community-management information is intended for authorized users.

Data deletion and questions

To ask a privacy question or request deletion of information associated with your Seva Hub account, contact aolnagawara@gmail.com.
